# T-011: Screen 11, the intelligence panel («الاستخبارات»)

## Goal
The dock's «الاستخبارات» (§6.5, §5.6) in the approved panel look, built with the shared builder and checker; generic agency names (D-13); no assassinations (D-14).

## Scope
- **In scope:** four tabs (نظرة عامة, الشبكات, العمليات, المكافحة), Egypt on 1 January 2011.
- **Out of scope:** the sheet that sets up a new operation (target, type, budget, risk preview): the next intelligence item; an exposure event (the event screens).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Same frame as the approved dock panels; identity purple on chrome only.
2. One tab body at a time; scrolling with fades and hint; every string shown clear in some view.
3. Estimates show their refresh month; operations within the capacity; 9 types, none a killing; no real agency name.
4. Colour never alone; every row and action read whole by VoiceOver.
5. Gate: 0 issues on 7 phones, the self-test catches every plant, `check_screen.py` PASS; two independent read-only reviews with every confirmed finding fixed.

## Still assumptions
All levels, budgets and operations are illustrative (§5.6 seeds them).
